How to use this with the kids
• Groups tab — the live league tables for all 12 groups. Green rows are heading to the knockouts.
• Teams & Cards tab — tap a flag to see the squad. Tap any player card to blow it up full-size.
• Bracket tab — the single-elimination road to the trophy.

What the card stats mean (and they're tuned to be fair, not random):
• HP — Stamina & engine. How much a player runs, presses and lasts. Box-to-box midfielders and flying fullbacks score high; veteran playmakers and pure poachers score lower.
• MP — Skill for their position. A keeper's MP is shot-stopping; a defender's is reading the game; a forward's is finishing & flair. So you can't directly compare a keeper's MP to a striker's — each is judged against their own job.
• Signature Move — the real-life thing that player is famous for, with a bit of fun.
• Card colour = rarity by skill: Legendary (gold), Epic (purple), Rare (blue), Common (grey).

Keeping it current: just say "update the World Cup tracker" after games. I'll pull the latest scores, recompute the tables, and advance the bracket. You don't have to type anything in yourself.
